Quick answers

Do I need an account?
No. LogLifted has no accounts and no sign-in. All your data stays on your device.
Where is my data stored? Is it backed up?
Everything is stored locally on your device. There is no cloud sync, so export regularly: Settings (gear icon) → Export Data saves a .lifted file you can keep in Files or iCloud Drive. Restore via Import Data.
The app crashed during a workout — did I lose my sets?
No. The active workout is auto-saved after every set and restored when you reopen the app.
How do I unlock Pro? Is it a subscription?
Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Tap Pro on the Home screen (or when you reach a free-tier limit) to unlock unlimited exercises/templates/plans, per-exercise rest timers, hidden exercises, the 1RM percentage table, and approximate workout calories.
I bought Pro but it isn't active.
Open Settings → Restore Purchases, then reopen the app if it doesn't refresh immediately.
Why does the app ask for Health access?
Only to read your latest body weight (to estimate workout calories) and to save completed workouts to Apple Health. It is optional — the app works fully without it, and your Health data never leaves your device.
