Privacy Policy

LogLifted — last updated 22 June 2026

Short version: LogLifted does not collect any of your data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. LogLifted has no backend and never transmits your workouts, exercises, plans, or any personal information to us or to any third party.

Where your data lives

All of your data (exercises, workouts, templates, plans, history, and settings) is stored locally on your device. You can export it yourself at any time as a .lifted file and import it on another device. Because there is no cloud backup, we recommend exporting periodically.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, LogLifted reads your most recent body weight from Apple Health to estimate calories burned during a workout, and can write your completed workouts to Apple Health. This information is exchanged only between the app and Apple Health on your device. It is never sent to us, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone. Health access is optional; the app works fully without it.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is processed by Apple through the App Store / StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, LogLifted schedules local reminders (such as the rest timer) entirely on your device. No notification data leaves the device.

Children

LogLifted does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
