LogLifted

A tool, not a fitness app — a precision strength log for people who lift.

LogLifted is a minimalist, local-first workout logger for athletes who already know what they're doing and just need to record their work — faster than pen and paper.

Why LogLifted

  • Zero friction. Weight and reps pre-fill from your last set; log a set in two taps.
  • Your data, your device. Everything stays local. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking, no ads.
  • Respect your time. No clutter, no feeds, no nagging.

What it does

  • Fast set logging with autofill and an automatic rest timer
  • Automatic personal-record detection and estimated 1RM
  • Supersets, reusable templates, and progression-based training plans (Push / Pull / Legs and more)
  • Full on-device workout history with a calendar overview
  • Import / export everything as a single .lifted file — share plans with a coach

Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription): it removes the free limits and adds per-exercise rest timers, hidden exercises, 1RM percentage tables, and approximate workout calories.
