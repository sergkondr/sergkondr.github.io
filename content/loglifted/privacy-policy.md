# Privacy Policy — LogLifted

**Last updated:** January 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction

LogLifted (“the App”) is a workout logging application designed to help users record and track their training.  
Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected, how it is used, and how it is stored.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

The App stores the data you manually enter, such as:

- exercises
- workouts
- sets, repetitions, and weights
- training templates or plans

This data is stored locally on your device.

---

### 2.2 Automatically collected information

The App may collect limited technical information using Apple-provided system services only, such as:

- crash reports
- basic diagnostic information

This information is used solely to improve stability and performance.  
No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

---

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

- provide the App’s functionality
- store and display your workouts and training data
- improve app stability and performance (crash reports, diagnostics)

The App does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All workout data is stored locally on your device.
- The App does not operate any external servers.
- In the future, optional iCloud backup may be added using Apple’s infrastructure.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share your personal data.

Data is only transmitted outside the device in the following cases:

- when you explicitly export your data (for example, as a JSON file)
- when Apple system services are used for crash reporting or diagnostics

No third-party services receive your workout data.

---

## 6. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time inside the App by removing exercises, workouts, templates, plans, and history.  
Deleting the App from your device also removes all locally stored data.

---

## 7. In-App Purchases

The App may offer one-time purchases in the future using Apple’s In-App Purchase system.  
All payments are processed by Apple. The App does not collect or store payment card information.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.  
All data entered into the App is provided directly by the user and stored locally on the device.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, you can contact us and we will address the issue.

---

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.  
Any changes will be published with a new “Last updated” date.
